WHO WE ARE

LOOMIX is an Indian clothing brand that combines utilitarian, concise and comfortable design. Our mission is to embody the dynamic rhythm of life and the freedom of creative people in original oversized clothing. In each model, we pay special attention to details, creating an original design that enriches your style. Our own production gives us the opportunity to be flexible and implement any ideas down to the smallest detail. Together with LOOMIX you can feel comfort, quality and self-expression in every look. Achieve freedom of choice and unsurpassedness with us
